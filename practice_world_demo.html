Does the same model get safer when you give it a practice world?

An AI bookkeeper reads invoices and either posts the journal entry or flags it for a human. Each answer is scored automatically against a known-correct key. Posting something that needed a human is counted as a confident-wrong error, the costly kind.

Results

Plain is a basic prompt. Engineered adds company rules and a "flag it, don't guess" instruction. Trained adds worked examples from a separate training set.

Look at the answers

The hard set has 48 documents with varied vendors, layouts and traps. Pick a case type, then a document, and compare what each model did.

Read these numbers with care. The documents are synthetic, each condition ran once, and the answer key is one person's judgment of what is correct. A few percentage points could be noise. This shows the approach works in a small test and does not show either model is ready for real books.
